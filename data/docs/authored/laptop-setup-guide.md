# Laptop Setup Guide

## First start

Charge the laptop to at least 50 percent, then hold the power button for two seconds. Follow the on-screen steps to choose language and Wi-Fi network.

## Backup

Create a recovery drive on a USB stick of at least 16 gigabytes before installing new software.

## Performance tips

Close unused applications and keep at least 15 percent of disk space free. Restart weekly to apply updates.

## Troubleshooting

If the laptop does not boot, hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a shutdown, then try again with only the charger connected.
