# Customer Privacy Policy

## Data we collect

We collect order history, delivery details, and review content. Payment card numbers are handled by our payment processor and are never stored by us.

## How data is used

Data is used to fulfil orders, provide support, and improve recommendations. Review text used for analytics is redacted of personal details.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. Requests are completed within 30 days.

## Retention

Order records are retained for 7 years for tax purposes. Support chat logs are deleted after 12 months.
