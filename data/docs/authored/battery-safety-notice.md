# Lithium Battery Safety Notice

## Charging

Use only the charger supplied with the device or a certified equivalent. Do not charge unattended overnight and never charge on soft surfaces such as beds.

## Storage

Store batteries at room temperature, away from heat and direct sunlight. Do not leave devices in a parked car in summer.

## Warning signs

Stop using the device immediately if the battery swells, overheats, leaks, or emits a smell. Do not attempt to open or puncture it.

## Disposal

Never throw lithium batteries in household waste. Return them to any store collection bin for free recycling.
