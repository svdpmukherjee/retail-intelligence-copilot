# Standard Warranty Terms

## Coverage period

Electronics carry a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date. Home appliances carry a 24-month warranty. Accessories and cables carry a 6-month warranty.

## What is covered

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failures under normal use. A defective unit is repaired or replaced at our discretion.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, cosmetic wear, and consumable parts such as batteries after 90 days are not covered.

## Making a claim

Submit a claim through the support portal with the order number and photos of the defect. Claims are reviewed within 3 business days.
