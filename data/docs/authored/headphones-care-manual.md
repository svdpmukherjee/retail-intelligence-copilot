# Headphones Care and Pairing Manual

## Pairing

Hold the power button for five seconds until the light flashes blue, then select the headphones in your device Bluetooth settings.

## Care

Wipe ear cushions with a dry cloth. Do not submerge the headphones; sweat resistance does not cover swimming or showers.

## Battery life

Expect up to 30 hours of playback with noise cancelling off and 20 hours with it on. A full charge takes 2 hours over USB-C.

## Volume safety

Listening above 85 decibels for extended periods can damage hearing. Use the built-in volume limiter for children.
