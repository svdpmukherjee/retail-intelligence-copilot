# Device Trade-In Program

## Eligible devices

Smartphones, tablets, and laptops less than 5 years old are eligible. Devices must power on and be free of activation locks.

## Valuation

Value depends on model, age, and condition. Cracked screens reduce the offer by 40 percent. Offers are valid for 14 days.

## Data wiping

Customers must factory reset the device before shipping it. We perform a certified data wipe on receipt but are not responsible for unbacked data.

## Payment

Trade-in value is paid as store credit within 3 business days of inspection.
