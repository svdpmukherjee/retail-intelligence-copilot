# Return and Refund Policy

## Return window

Most items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Electronics may be returned within 15 days. Books and media may be returned within 30 days if unopened or in new condition.

## Condition requirements

Items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals. Items showing signs of wear, damage by the customer, or missing parts are eligible only for a partial refund of up to 50 percent.

## Refund method and timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was sent in error.

## Non-returnable items

Opened software, personal care items, perishable goods, gift cards, and custom-made products cannot be returned unless they arrive defective.
