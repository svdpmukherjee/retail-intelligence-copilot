# Customer Support Service Levels

## Response times

Email enquiries receive a first response within 24 hours. Live chat is answered within 2 minutes during opening hours, 8am to 8pm on weekdays.

## Escalation

Unresolved cases are escalated to a senior agent after 3 business days. Safety issues are escalated immediately.

## Compensation

If a delivery promise is missed by more than 3 days, customers receive a 10 dollar credit.
