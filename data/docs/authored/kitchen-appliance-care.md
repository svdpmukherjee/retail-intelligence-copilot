# Kitchen Appliance Care Manual

## Blenders

Never run a blender empty or over its maximum fill line. Clean the jar after each use and do not wash the motor base in water.

## Air fryers

Remove the basket and wash it by hand with warm soapy water. Do not use metal utensils on the non-stick coating.

## Descaling

Descale coffee machines every 3 months using the manufacturer's descaling solution. Vinegar is not recommended as it can damage seals.

## Electrical safety

Unplug appliances before cleaning. Never use a damaged cord and do not operate appliances near sinks.
