# Shipping and Delivery Guide

## Delivery options

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days. Same-day delivery is available in selected metropolitan areas for orders placed before noon.

## Shipping fees

Standard shipping is free on orders over 50 dollars; otherwise it costs 4.99 dollars. Express shipping costs 12.99 dollars. Oversized items carry a 29.99 dollar freight surcharge.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed once the order ships. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Lost or delayed parcels

If a parcel has not arrived 10 business days after shipping, contact support. We will open a carrier investigation and either resend the order or refund it in full.
