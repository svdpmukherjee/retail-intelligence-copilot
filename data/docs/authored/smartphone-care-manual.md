# Smartphone Care Manual

## Cleaning

Power off the phone and wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth lightly dampened with water. Do not use bleach, abrasive cleaners, or compressed air.

## Water resistance

Water resistance is rated IP68 for up to 1.5 metres for 30 minutes in fresh water when new. Resistance may decrease with age and is not covered by the warranty.

## Software updates

Install updates when prompted. Keep at least 20 percent battery and 1 gigabyte of free storage before updating.

## Battery health

Avoid draining the battery to zero. Battery capacity is considered degraded when it falls below 80 percent of its original capacity.
