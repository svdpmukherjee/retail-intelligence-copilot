# Customer Review Moderation Guidelines

## Allowed content

Reviews may describe personal experience with a product, its quality, and its value. Constructive criticism is welcome.

## Removed content

Reviews containing personal information, hate speech, spam, or promotion of other sellers are removed. Reviews about shipping only are labelled and excluded from product ratings.

## Verified purchase badge

The Verified Purchase badge appears only when the reviewer bought the product through our store.

## Incentivised reviews

Reviews written in exchange for free products must disclose the incentive and are marked accordingly.
