# Installation and Assembly Services

## Available services

Installation is available for large appliances, televisions over 50 inches, and flat-pack furniture. Booking is made at checkout or within 7 days of delivery.

## Pricing

Appliance installation costs 49 dollars, television wall mounting 79 dollars, and furniture assembly 39 dollars per item.

## Cancellations

Appointments can be rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before the slot. Later changes incur a 15 dollar fee.
