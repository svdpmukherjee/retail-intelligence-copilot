# Used Book Condition Grading

## Grades

Like New means no visible wear. Very Good means minor shelf wear with no markings. Good means some wear and light highlighting. Acceptable means heavy wear but all pages intact.

## Guarantee

A book that does not match its stated grade may be returned within 30 days for a full refund including shipping.

## Digital codes

Access codes bundled with used textbooks are not guaranteed to be unused and are not eligible for refunds.
