# Product Recall Procedure

## Notification

Affected customers are notified by email and phone within 48 hours of a recall decision. Notices are also posted on the website and in stores.

## Customer actions

Stop using the recalled product and check the batch number against the recall list. Do not return the product to a store before contacting support unless instructed.

## Remedy

Customers receive a free replacement, repair, or full refund, chosen by the customer where stock allows. No proof of purchase is required for recalled items.

## Internal escalation

Any store associate who suspects a safety defect must report it to the Quality team the same day.
