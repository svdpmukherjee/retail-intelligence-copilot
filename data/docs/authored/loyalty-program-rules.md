# Loyalty Program Rules

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point per dollar spent on eligible purchases. Double points apply during promotional weeks. Gift cards and taxes do not earn points.

## Tiers

The Silver tier starts at 0 points, Gold at 1,000 points per year, and Platinum at 5,000 points per year. Gold members get free express shipping; Platinum members also get early access to sales.

## Redeeming points

100 points redeem for a 5 dollar discount. Points expire 12 months after they are earned if the account has no activity.

## Account misuse

Points earned through fraudulent returns or abuse of promotions will be removed and the membership may be suspended.
