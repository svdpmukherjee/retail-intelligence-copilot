# Price Match Policy

## Eligibility

We match the advertised price of an identical in-stock item from an authorized retailer within 14 days of purchase.

## Exclusions

Clearance items, marketplace sellers, auction sites, bundle offers, and limited-time flash sales are excluded from price matching.

## How to request

Send the competitor link or ad together with your order number to support. Approved adjustments are credited to the original payment method within 5 business days.
